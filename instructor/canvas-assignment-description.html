Problem Set 3: Does More Recent Data Lead to a Better Portfolio?

Release: Sunday, October 4, 2026
Initial submission deadline: Sunday, October 18, 2026, at 11:59 PM ET
Maximum score: 4 points

Invest USD 10,000 in eight stocks and decide how much past data to use. Estimate growth rates and covariances from two windows: all 2014–2025 prices and 2025 prices alone. For each window, choose a long-only and a shorts-allowed global minimum-variance portfolio. Then hold all four portfolios over the same 126 trading days in 2026, with borrowing rates of 0% and 3% on short positions, and compare them with an equal-weight reference and a 5% benchmark. Advanced also fits a multiple-asset geometric Brownian motion (MAGBM) model to each window and estimates each portfolio's probability of beating the benchmark.
